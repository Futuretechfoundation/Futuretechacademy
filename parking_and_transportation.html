Parking & Transportation

FutureTech Academy is conveniently located in downtown Rochester at 221 1st Ave SW, Suite 610, Rochester, MN 55902. We offer several parking and transportation options to make your visit simple and accessible.

Parking Information

  • Visitor Parking: Available at the 1st Street Parking Ramp and 2nd Street Parking Ramp, both within a short walking distance of our building.
  • Student & Faculty Parking: Accessible via nearby parking ramps with monthly permit options.
  • Accessible Parking: Handicap-accessible parking spaces are available in all city ramps close to the Academy entrance.
  • Electric Vehicle Charging: EV charging stations available in the 2nd Street Ramp and 1st Avenue Ramp.

Transportation Options

  • Public Transit: Rochester Public Transit (RPT) routes 17 and 18 stop near 2nd Street SW, just a short walk from our building.
  • Bicycle Parking: Bike racks are available along 1st Avenue SW and 2nd Street SW.
  • Ride-Share Services: Designated drop-off and pick-up zones are available near the 1st Avenue entrance.
  • Walking Access: The Academy is connected to the downtown skyway and subway system for all-weather access.
